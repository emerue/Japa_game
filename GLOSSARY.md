# JAPA Game

An open-world Nigerian life game about one person's attempt to emigrate ("japa") or to build a good life at home, set in an explorable Lagos and built on real-world obstacles.

## Language

**Japa**:
Nigerian slang for emigrating. In this game it is one possible goal, not the only good outcome.
_Avoid_: Migration, relocation (in player-facing text)

### Structure of play

**Run**:
One life, from choosing a Background to reaching an Ending, played in the same World as every other Run.
_Avoid_: Journey, game, session, playthrough

**Act**:
One of the five phases of a Run: Lagos Grind, Processing, The Agent Market, The Gate, The Life You Actually Get.
_Avoid_: Stage, level, chapter

**Event**:
A situation the player responds to, triggered by a place, an NPC, an object or the phone.
_Avoid_: Card, scenario, encounter

**Choice**:
One of the two to four responses to an Event, each showing its known costs.
_Avoid_: Option, answer, swipe

**Quest**:
An objective made of linked Events, either the main migration storyline or an optional side activity.
_Avoid_: Mission, task

**Ending**:
How a Run concludes, including the losses Deep Debt and Burnout. Staying in Nigeria can be a winning Ending.
_Avoid_: Game over, result

**JAPA Receipt**:
The shareable, spoiler-safe card produced from a Run's decisions and Ending.
_Avoid_: Result card, score card

### The player

**Background**:
The circumstances a player starts a Run with, such as Fresh Graduate, which set starting Resources and story access.
_Avoid_: Archetype, trait, class, character

**Resource**:
One of the five quantities Choices change: Funds, Energy, Readiness, Home ties and Risk sense. Only Funds and Energy can end a Run.
_Avoid_: Stat, meter, bar, Sanity

**Funds**:
The player's fictional available money.
_Avoid_: Money, Naira, cash

**Energy**:
The player's time, stamina and emotional bandwidth.
_Avoid_: Sanity, stress, health

**Readiness**:
How prepared, verified and complete the player's plan is.
_Avoid_: Documents, progress

**Home ties**:
The player's relationship with family: support, expectations and obligations, which can help or cost.
_Avoid_: Family Pressure, Family Support, black tax (as a Resource name)

**Risk sense**:
The player's ability to question claims and verify offers.
_Avoid_: Scam meter, awareness

### The world

**World**:
The fixed, persistent explorable map shared by every Run.
_Avoid_: Map (for the whole world), level

**Zone**:
A connected area of the World anchored on one Landmark.
_Avoid_: District (for a single area), scene, level

**Landmark**:
A real Lagos place, such as Ojuelegba or Third Mainland Bridge, that anchors a Zone or appears on its horizon.
_Avoid_: Location, POI

**Interactable**:
A person, door, object or service the player can act on. Anything marked interactable must do something.
_Avoid_: Hotspot, prop (for interactive things)

**Abroad hub**:
The compact explorable destination (a shared flat and one street) where Act 5 abroad is played.
_Avoid_: Abroad level, destination scene

### Where the player is going

**Destination**:
The country the player is trying to reach.

**Route**:
A specific legal way into a Destination, such as UK Student, with its own requirements and Events. Stay and Build is the Route that stays in Lagos.
_Avoid_: Pathway, visa type

**The Gate**:
The high-pressure checkpoint of Act 4 that tests whether the player's plan, evidence and answers are consistent.
_Avoid_: Embassy minigame, visa lottery

**Refusal**:
A decision at The Gate that goes against the player. It is a setback with a cause, not automatically the end of a Run.
_Avoid_: Rejection, denial

### Social

**Presence**:
Seeing and interacting with other real players' avatars in the World, through emotes and preset phrases only.
_Avoid_: Multiplayer, MMO, chat

**Share seed**:
A link that lets a friend play the same starting dilemma and compare outcomes.
_Avoid_: Challenge code, referral link
