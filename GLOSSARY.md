# JAPA Game

A satirical, choice-driven browser game about one Nigerian's attempt to emigrate, built on real-world obstacles.

## Language

**Japa**:
Nigerian slang for emigrating abroad. In this game it is the player's goal: to leave Nigeria, arrive in a destination country and settle there.
_Avoid_: Migration, relocation (in player-facing text)

### Structure of play

**Run**:
One playthrough, from choosing an Archetype to reaching an Ending.
_Avoid_: Journey (reserved for the dev log), game, session

**Stage**:
One phase of a Run: Hustle, Paperwork, Visa Interview, Departure, Settling In, always in that order.
_Avoid_: Level, chapter

**Event**:
A single situation the player must respond to, drawn from its Stage's set of Events.
_Avoid_: Card, scenario

**Choice**:
One of the responses available for an Event. Some Choices are only available once the player meets a requirement.
_Avoid_: Option, answer

**Ending**:
How a Run concludes. Only one Ending is a win (settling in); all others are losses, including giving up.
_Avoid_: Game over, result

### The player

**Archetype**:
The kind of person the player plays as, which sets their starting Meters.
_Avoid_: Character, class

**Meter**:
One of four resources every Choice can raise or lower: **Naira** (money), **Sanity** (stress), **Family Support** (goodwill and backing) and **Time** (how long before the chance passes). Any Meter reaching zero ends the Run.
_Avoid_: Stat, resource, bar

**Credential**:
A document or proof the player collects, such as a passport or proof of funds. A Credential is held or not held; it has no amount.
_Avoid_: Document, item

### Where the player is going

**Destination**:
The country the player is trying to reach.

**Route**:
A specific legal way into a Destination, such as a Student Visa, with its own required Credentials and Events.
_Avoid_: Pathway, visa type

**Refusal**:
A visa decision that goes against the player because Credentials are missing. It sends them back to Paperwork; a second Refusal ends the Run.
_Avoid_: Rejection, denial
