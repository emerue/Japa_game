SAT 17 OCT 2026 (afternoon): 3D spike and look tests
====================================================================
3D spike (Japa_game/spikes/3d-street/, Three.js r170 vendored in
Japa_game/vendor/three/)
- Ojuelegba-style junction, 20 buildings, 6 animated characters on the
  Infinix HOT 40i. Follow camera: 60 fps either way. Whole junction on
  screen at full resolution: 60 fps (shadows off), 55 fps with p95 frame
  33.5 ms (shadows on). Gate passed; ADR 0001 accepted.
- Two mistakes along the way, both fixed: the first camera framing showed
  almost nothing (so the numbers didn't count), and adb dropped URL
  params after "&" until the URL was quoted for the phone's shell.

Look test (look.html), from @ikenna's feedback: "camera too far,
characters very small, danfo doesn't look like a danfo, characters don't
look human"
- The 50° camera mostly shows tarmac and roofs in portrait. A lower,
  closer camera (~32°, 9 m) puts the player in the lower-middle with the
  street and shopfronts filling the frame, leaving the bottom strip for
  controls. Buildings between camera and player fade. Vehicles must fade
  too (a danfo blocked the view once).
- Danfo rebuilt in code: two black stripes, window band, open sliding
  door with seats, roof rack with luggage, bumpers, lights. Still needs a
  rounded nose, a conductor and slogans; waiting on @ikenna's review.

Characters (chars.html)
- @ikenna downloaded RG Poly Cartoon City (free) and Quaternius Ultimate
  Modular Men/Women. RG Poly's zip uses LZMA: macOS unzip and ditto
  can't open it, bsdtar can.
- Quaternius chosen: real proportions, plain-colour skin materials (easy
  to recolour), 24 animations. RG Poly rejected (chibi, white skin baked
  into the texture).
- Seven warm skin tones, all used. Deep tones lost their faces under one
  sun; fixed with a warm fill light, a back light, a skin glow that grows
  with depth of tone, and dark glossy pupils. Eye whites still to add.
- Found: the Suit model holds a pistol; some hairstyles don't fit Lagos.
  Both to be removed or replaced in the slice.

Design review
- @ikenna and Claude went through PRD v3 (open world) question by question
  (Q1–Q31). Outcome recorded in PRD.md §0. Highlights:
  - v3 is the product source of truth; the old PRD.md and the v3 source

  - Resources: Funds, Energy, Readiness, Home ties, Risk sense. Only Funds

  - Sound on by default at low volume, with a visible mute.

Build
- Built the first playable version in Japa_game/: Fresh Graduate on the
  UK Student Visa route, five stages (Hustle, Paperwork, Visa Interview,
  Departure, Settling In), 27 events, 9 endings.
- Engine: pure functions over an immutable run state, seeded RNG, all
  content as data. Includes a content validator and a balance simulator.
  Over 5,000 simulated runs, a sensible bot wins about 88% of the time and
  a random bot about 5%.
- Tests: 17 passing (node --test). They cover choices, requirements, visa
  refusal and retry, endings, determinism, and 500 full runs that all
  reach a valid ending.
- No dependencies. Run it with `npm run dev`, then open http://localhost:5173.

Feedback
- @ikenna rejected the presentation: "a text based question and answer
  game". The engine and content approach carry over; the presentation is
  being redone.

Problem: npm install fails
- npm fails with UNABLE_TO_GET_ISSUER_CERT_LOCALLY, even outside the
  sandbox. Likely a machine-level TLS/proxy issue; cause not found yet.
- Workaround: plain JavaScript with JSDoc types for now. Not needed until
  Phase 2 (Vite, TypeScript, Workbox).

Problem: the dev Mac can't render 3D
- Hardware: MacBook Pro (mid-2015, MacBookPro11,5) with Intel Iris Pro and
  AMD Radeon R9 M370X, running macOS 15.5 through OpenCore Legacy Patcher
  2.4.1.
- WebGL fails in both Chrome and Safari. chrome://gpu shows the GPU
  process not starting (GPU0 VENDOR=0x0000). get.webgl.org draws no cube.
  The patched graphics drivers are loaded, but 3D isn't accelerated.
- Consequence: test 3D on a phone over local Wi-Fi (D5). The Mac becomes
  the lite-view test device.

Research: Lagos Life (lagoslife.app), hands-on review plus screenshots
- 3D world built from simple shapes: flat colours, no textures, a tilted
  overhead camera. Interiors are cutaway dollhouse rooms (e.g. Marina
  Towers).
- Characters are animated models with name tags; other real players show
  up in shared rooms.
- Frosted UI floats over the world: a top bar (date, mood, players online,
  money +), quest chips, compact needs bars, and a bottom tab bar (Home,
  Buy, Map, Phone).
- Events pop up as cards with an illustration and 2–3 buttons, with the
  cost shown on each, plus a free way out.
- The in-game phone is the hub. Sponsor apps (Heala, PopOut Tickets) sit
  there, and a Fireboy DML release runs as a scavenger hunt.
- Billboards across the map are ad slots ("YOUR AD HERE ₦250k / 7 days").
- When 3D fails, it falls back to a lite 2D view and tells the player.
- Result: proposed direction in PRD §6, awaiting decisions D1–D5.
